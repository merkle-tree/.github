### AI agents that get work done for your MSP

**Technician agent**: resolves tickets.
- **Helpdesk resolution**: diagnoses support requests, applies approved fixes, verifies them and documents the work in your PSA.
- **Vulnerability remediation**: prioritizes exposed devices, installs approved patches and rechecks them.
- **Backup recovery**: fixes failed backups, reruns the job and verifies the new restore point.

**Growth agent**: wins new clients.
- **Signal-based prospecting**: watches for new offices, breaches, IT hires and other signals, and matches companies to your ideal client.
- **Personalized sequencing**: researches decision-makers, writes outreach, follows up and books qualified meetings.
- **AI search optimization**: measures and improves how often AI assistants recommend you.

**Back-office agent**: keeps operations moving.
- **Billing reconciliation**: catches unbilled usage, wrong quantities and pricing mismatches.
- **Quote generation**: drafts scopes and quotes from discovery notes for your approval.
- **Client reporting**: turns tickets, backup health and security findings into client reports.

### Works on your terms

- **Learns from your work**: verified resolutions, campaign results and meeting notes feed back into the agents.
- **Human judgment where it matters**: you set the boundaries, and agents escalate with context when they're unsure or need approval.
- **Reliable rules, flexible reasoning**: rules enforce your procedures and permissions, and agents reason within them.
